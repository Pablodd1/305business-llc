MACALOCATOR

AI-powered mobile auto service & roadside assistance platform

A deployed 'we come to you' auto service platform — customers book mobile mechanics to their driveway, office, or roadside position. Live unit-tracking map, one-tap 24/7 emergency dispatch (tow, jump start, accident), Gemini AI concierge chat with voice in/out, voice command controller, multi-vehicle garage with maintenance reminders, Google Calendar sync, AI marketing creative studio, and a 5-tab admin command center with analytics and Twilio-ready comms. Production demo live.

LIVE DEMO
THE PAIN

Stranded drivers, missed calls, greasy shops

When a car dies, the experience is awful: phone tag with tow companies, opaque pricing, hours at a greasy shop, no idea when help arrives. And the operators on the other side run dispatch from phone calls and whiteboards. MacaLocator fixes both sides — customers get one-tap dispatch with live ETAs and AI concierge support; operators get a live unit map, booking pipeline, and a 24/7 AI front desk.

INSIDE THE PLATFORM — MODULE BY MODULE

Exactly what each module does in the running product — no vaporware, no mockups.

01 — CLIENT DASHBOARD & LIVE DISPATCH MAP
Real-time unit tracking — every mobile unit on a live map with status (En Route / On Call) and live ETA to the active request
Map intelligence filters — all units / team only / active requests, tap any point for the full unit dossier
Booking pipeline control — confirm, start, and complete jobs; booking moves pending → confirmed → in progress → completed in real time
Instant quote estimator — picks vehicle + service for an on-the-spot price; detects electric vehicles and adapts pricing logic for EV powertrains
02 — ON-DEMAND BOOKING ENGINE
Service catalog cards — each service ships with description, pricing basis, and one-tap select
Doorstep dispatch addressing — the customer confirms home, office, or hotel parking and the mechanic comes to them
Date & time scheduling with full lifecycle status tracking on every booking
03 — 24/7 EMERGENCY ROADSIDE RESPONSE
One-tap emergency grid — tow truck, accident help, jump start, or share-my-location, one tap from any screen
Live dispatch choreography — request → locating nearest unit → dispatched with confirmation and direct call action
Visible from both sides — emergencies surface on the operations map and admin console for coordinator handoff
04 — GEMINI AI CONCIERGE CHAT — VOICE IN, VOICE OUT
A Gemini concierge answers service questions, quotes pricing, explains coverage zones, and walks customers into a booking — in the buyer's brand voice
Voice dictation input — customers dictate by microphone instead of typing
Spoken readback — every AI answer read aloud with natural speech synthesis, toggleable
Never dead-ends — degrades to canned concierge answers if the API is unreachable
05 — VOICE COMMAND CONTROLLER
Hands-free operation — browser-native speech recognition drives the app by voice, with spoken confirmations
Full permission and error handling — microphone-denied states, no-recognition retries, sound controls
06 — GARAGE & VEHICLE PROFILES
Multi-vehicle garage — add/remove vehicles with make, model, year, plate, and live mileage
Maintenance memory — per-vehicle oil and tire baselines with notification preferences
EV-aware logic — electric vehicles detected and handled correctly (battery health checks, no oil reminders)
Editable profile with service history tied to each vehicle
07 — GOOGLE CALENDAR SYNC
One-click Google OAuth connect with live connected state and event listing
Bookings become real calendar events — no-show reduction built in
08 — AI MARKETING CREATIVE STUDIO
Text-to-image generation of flyers, social creatives, and promo visuals from plain prompts
8 aspect ratios — 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 9:16, 16:9, 21:9, 3:2, 2:3 — every placement from Instagram post to billboard
The marketing team a towing company never had
09 — ADMIN & FRONT DESK COMMAND CENTER
Operations analytics — revenue, job, and KPI cards with mini visualizations
Live visualization tab — the full dispatch map, reimagined for coordinators
AI front desk — configurable automated reception, day or night
Twilio-ready comms — SMS/voice layer wired for notifications and dispatch alerts
Dedicated communications tab — dispatch, mechanics, and front desk on one thread
