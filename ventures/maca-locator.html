WHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES — EVERY MODULE, EVERY DETAIL

This is not a landing page with screenshots. It's a working multi-sided platform: a consumer app, an operations console, and an AI layer. Below is exactly what each module does in the running demo.

01 — CLIENT DASHBOARD & LIVE DISPATCH MAP
Real-time unit tracking. Every mobile unit rendered on a live map with name, zone, vehicle type, equipment payload ("high-voltage battery scanner & tire calibration toolkit"), current status — En Route, Ready & On Call — and live ETA to the active request.
Map intelligence filters. Filter the board by all units / team only / active requests, tap any point for the full unit dossier.
Booking pipeline control. Confirm, start, and complete jobs directly from the dashboard — the booking moves pending → confirmed → in progress → completed in real time.
Instant quote estimator. Pick a vehicle + service and get an on-the-spot price. The estimator detects electric vehicles (Tesla, Bolt…) and adapts the service menu and pricing logic for EV powertrains automatically.
02 — ON-DEMAND BOOKING ENGINE
Service catalog cards. Each service (tire rotation, battery diagnostic, mobile diagnostics, wash & detailing…) ships with its own description, pricing basis, and one-tap select.
Doorstep dispatch addressing. The customer never drives anywhere — they confirm the service address (home, office, hotel parking) and the mechanic comes to them. That's the whole business model, encoded.
Date & time scheduling with full lifecycle status tracking on every booking.
03 — 24/7 EMERGENCY ROADSIDE RESPONSE
One-tap emergency grid. Tow truck, accident help, jump start, or "other — share my location", always one tap away from any screen (pinned in the sidebar on desktop, red button on mobile).
Live dispatch choreography. Request → "Locating nearest unit…" → unit dispatched with confirmation screen and direct call action. The exact emotional flow a stranded driver needs.
Visible from both sides. Emergency requests surface on the operations map and in the admin console for coordinator handoff.
04 — GEMINI AI CONCIERGE CHAT (VOICE IN, VOICE OUT)
GPT-class support desk, branded. A Gemini 3.1 Flash concierge answers service questions, quotes pricing, explains coverage zones, and walks customers into a booking — in the buyer's brand voice.
Voice dictation input. Customers can dictate their message by microphone instead of typing.
Spoken readback. Every AI answer is read aloud with natural speech synthesis — toggleable — and markdown is stripped for clean audio.
Never dead-ends. If the API is unreachable the chat degrades to a canned concierge instead of hanging — your customers always get an answer.
05 — VOICE COMMAND CONTROLLER
Hands-free operation. Browser-native speech recognition lets users drive the app by voice; the app talks back with spoken confirmations. Built for drivers, literally.
Permission & error handling. Microphone-denied states, no-recognition retries, and sound on/off controls are all handled.
06 — GARAGE & VEHICLE PROFILES
Multi-vehicle garage. Add/remove vehicles with make, model, year, license plate, and live mileage per vehicle.
Maintenance memory. Per-vehicle oil-change and tire-rotation baselines with notification preferences — the app knows when each car is due before the customer does.
EV-aware logic. Electric vehicles are detected and handled correctly (no oil reminders on a Tesla — battery health checks instead).
Profile management. Editable name, email, phone; service history ties back to the vehicle it was performed on.
07 — GOOGLE CALENDAR SYNC
OAuth connect. One-click Google account connection with live "connected" state and event listing inside the profile.
Bookings become calendar events. A scheduled service can push a real calendar event to the customer's Google Calendar — no-show reduction built in.
08 — AI MARKETING CREATIVE STUDIO
Text-to-image generation. Gemini image model generates flyers, social creatives, and promo visuals from a plain-English prompt.
8 aspect ratios — 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 9:16, 16:9, 21:9, 3:2, 2:3 — covering every placement from Instagram post to billboard.
Built for the operator. The marketing team of a towing company is usually zero people. This module is the marketing team.
09 — ADMIN & FRONT DESK COMMAND CENTER
Operations analytics. Revenue, job, and KPI cards with mini visualizations — the owner view of the whole business.
Live visualization tab. The full dispatch map, reimagined for coordinators.
AI front desk. Configurable automated reception — the first line that answers every inbound, day or night.
Twilio-ready comms. SMS / voice messaging layer wired for customer notifications and dispatch alerts.
Dedicated communications tab. Centralized staff comms so dispatch, mechanics, and front desk stay on one thread.

HOW IT MAKES MONEY

Per-service dispatch margin. Every booking and emergency call is a billable dispatch — the estimator sets the price, the platform records the job.
Membership subscriptions. Roadside clubs charge monthly for peace of mind; this platform manages the members, the units, and the dispatches.
Fleet & B2B contracts. Corporate parking lots, rental fleets, and dealerships buy guaranteed response SLAs — the admin console reports on all of it.
White-label licensing. Sell the platform itself to operators in other cities — the rebrand path is a config, not a rewrite.
